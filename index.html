About Us

A Story of Passion & Flavor

Maison Braise opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: cook everything over live fire, source what's close by, and let people linger at the table.

What began as a small neighborhood grill has grown into a gathering place — but the kitchen still runs on the same rhythm: market runs at dawn, slow braises through the afternoon, and the last plate going out well after midnight.

  • Fresh local ingredients
  • Traditional recipes, reimagined
  • Warm & cozy atmosphere
